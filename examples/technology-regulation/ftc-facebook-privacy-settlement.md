In 2019, the FTC imposed a $5 billion penalty and extensive privacy-governance requirements on Facebook for violating a prior consent order and misleading users about data controls. The case was significant because it used enforcement not only to punish privacy failures, but also to require ongoing changes in corporate oversight and executive accountability.

The post-agentic AI vocabulary describes the newer set of terms used to discuss leading AI systems, including frontier models, multimodality, instruction-following, agentic coding, and long-horizon tasks. These buzzwords reflect a shift from models that mainly generate responses toward systems that can work across different media, follow complex directions, and carry out extended tasks with greater autonomy.
